Overview

Short version: ShikshaVega collects only the data needed to run your school. We do not sell your data. We do not show ads. Your school's data is yours.

This Privacy Policy applies to the ShikshaVega mobile application (Android), the ShikshaVega web portal, and all related services provided by Soir Software Solutions ("we", "us", "our"). By using ShikshaVega, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

ShikshaVega serves multiple types of users — School Administrators, Teachers, Students, and Parents/Guardians. This policy covers data collected from all these roles.

Section 04

How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to provide and improve the ShikshaVega service. Specifically:

  • To create and manage your account on ShikshaVega
  • To provide core ERP features — attendance, fees, grades, timetable, notices
  • To send important school notifications via push alerts, SMS, or WhatsApp
  • To process fee payments and generate receipts
  • To generate academic reports and report cards for students
  • To enable school-to-parent communication through the app
  • To provide customer support when you contact us
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues or abuse
  • To improve app performance and user experience based on usage patterns
  • To comply with applicable Indian laws and regulations

We do not use your data for advertising. ShikshaVega is a paid B2B SaaS platform — we have no reason or incentive to monetise your data.

Section 06

Storage & Security

All ShikshaVega data is stored on secure cloud servers located in India. We implement the following security measures:

  • All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher (HTTPS everywhere)
  • All data at rest is encrypted using AES-256 encryption
  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt — we cannot see your password
  • Role-based access control — users only see data relevant to their role
  • Regular automated backups with point-in-time recovery
  • Two-factor authentication (2FA) available for Admin accounts
  • Security audits and penetration testing conducted periodically
  • Strict internal access controls — only authorized Soir engineers can access infrastructure

Data residency: Your school's data is stored in servers located within India, in compliance with applicable Indian data protection regulations.

Section 07

Children's Privacy

ShikshaVega is used in schools and therefore processes data of students who may be under 18 years of age. We take this responsibility seriously:

  • Student accounts are created and managed by the school, not directly by the student
  • Parents and guardians are linked to student accounts and receive all notifications
  • Students under 13 do not interact directly with the platform without guardian involvement
  • We collect only the minimum data necessary for academic management of each student
  • Student data is never used for advertising, profiling, or shared outside the school context
  • Schools (as data controllers) are responsible for obtaining appropriate consent from parents

Parents: You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your child's data by contacting your school's administrator or emailing us directly at privacy@soirsoftware.com.

Section 10

Third-Party Services

ShikshaVega integrates with a small number of trusted third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google) — Push notifications to the mobile app
  • Razorpay / PayU — Online fee payment processing (PCI-DSS certified)
  • Twilio / MSG91 — SMS alerts sent to parents and staff
  • Google Maps — Transport route display and bus tracking
  • AWS S3 — Secure storage of uploaded documents and photos

Each of these providers is contractually bound to process data only as directed by us and in accordance with applicable privacy laws. Links to the ShikshaVega website or app may lead to third-party websites — we are not responsible for their privacy practices.

Section 11

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your school's account is active with ShikshaVega.

Data Type Retention Period
Student academic records For the duration of enrollment + 3 years after leaving the school
Fee payment records 7 years (as required by Indian financial regulations)
Staff employment records Duration of employment + 3 years
App usage logs 90 days, then automatically deleted
All school data after account closure Deleted within 60 days of account termination (export available)

Schools can export all their data at any time from the Admin Dashboard. Upon account termination, we provide a 30-day window to export before deletion.
